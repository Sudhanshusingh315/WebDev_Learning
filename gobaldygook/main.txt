3 searching in VIM

1 built into vim are several ways to search for text, all must be accessed from normal mode.
1 / will allow us to search in a forwrd direction from where we currently are
2 n will find the next occurrence of that word before wrapping around
3 N will find the previous occurrence of that word before wrapping around

4 ? allows us to search in a backward direction from where we currently are
5 n will find the next BACKWARD occurrence of that word (flipped from its usage in /)
6 N will find the previous BACKWARD occurrence of the word (flipped from its usage in ?)
7 // will repeat the previous back search (same idea as /)

10 * will search for the next instance of the word we are currently on
11 # will search for the previous instance of the word we are currently on

13 but how do we find and replace text?
14 :%s/find/replace/options  (g replaces globally, gi replaces globally ignoring case, gc replaces globally but prompts before each replacement)
